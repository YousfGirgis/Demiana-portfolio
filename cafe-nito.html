Cafe-Nito

A bold cafe concept translating the qualities of Ivo Otasevic's “a” Chair into spatial rules, contrast, movement, and atmosphere.

Concept

Chair logic becomes a cafe language.

The project begins with Ivo Otasevic's chair, an everyday object that also reads as an art installation. Its combination of wood, plastic, metal, color, and sculptural form informed the cafe's spatial approach.

The design translates chair qualities into rules of changing direction, orientation, ribbon-like continuity, and contrast. Neon green, glossy black, and glass emphasize the cafe's boldness while the layout balances circulation and seating.
