Pentagram Office

A multidisciplinary workplace concept in New York City for architecture, interior, and graphic design teams.

Workplace

Three floors for focus, collaboration, and presentation.

Pentagram Office spans three floors with flexible workstations, client presentation rooms, libraries, show room areas, model making, focus rooms, and private offices.

Custom elements such as the reception desk and platform stairs reinforce brand identity, while wood, glass, and acoustic finishes balance aesthetics and performance. The concept focuses on collaboration, sustainability, and workplace wellness.
