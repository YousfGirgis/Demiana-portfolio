Tiny Suite

A compact living space designed around comfort, accessibility, and a careful human-scale response to limited square footage.

Compact Living

Small space, tuned proportions.

Tiny Suite explores a compact living space tailored for a person 5 feet tall. The challenge was to maximize comfort and efficiency within a limited footprint by adjusting proportions, furniture scale, circulation, storage, and ceiling height.

The result balances functionality, intimacy, and adaptability, showing how small spaces can still feel comfortable and thoughtfully designed.
