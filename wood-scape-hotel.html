Wood Scape Hotel

A biophilic hospitality concept designed as an urban retreat for comfort, wellness, and visual connection with nature.

Hospitality

An urban hotel shaped by biophilic patterns.

Wood Scape Hotel was designed for the Urban Biophilic Design Competition. Guided by the 14 Patterns of Biophilic Design, the concept emphasizes visual connection with nature, material connection, refuge, natural light, warm textures, and calm spatial layouts.

The project incorporates WELL V2 building standards with attention to air and water quality, daylight, acoustics, and wellness-driven amenities. The program includes a lobby, restaurant and cafe, gym, pool, and 80 guest rooms.

  • Biophilic design
  • WELL V2 standards
  • 80 guest rooms
  • Hospitality amenities
